Welcome to the World of Volleyball!

Get ready to serve, spike, and dig your way to victory!

Volleyball is a team sport where two teams of six players are separated by a high net and bat a ball back and forth using their hands or other body parts, aiming to land it on the opponent's court. Core Gameplay & Rules • Team Structure: Each team has six players on the court at a time (three in the front row and three in the back row), who rotate clockwise every time they win back service. • Ball Contacts: A team is allowed a maximum of three touches to return the ball across the net (not including a block touch). • Scoring & Winning: Played to best-of-five sets, points are scored on every rally (rally point system) until a team reaches 25 points (and leads by at least 2) for sets 1 through 4, or 15 points for the deciding 5th set. • Specialist Roles: Includes the libero, a back-row defensive specialist who wears a contrasting jersey and does not rotate to the front row.
